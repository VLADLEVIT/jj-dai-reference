#07 KIND: COMPOSITION STATUSprev: #06

The honest sentence

what changed · what must not be overclaimed

v0.4.1 closed the identity gap. v0.5-dev5 closed the cryptographic boundary. v0.5-dev6 made replication sound. v0.5.1 closed P1 mechanisms. v0.5.2 added the Monero anchor. v0.5.3 closed the COMPOSITION GAP: one BeingRuntime drives every organ through one witnessed lifecycle over one identity, and POST /v1/tasks returns the full DecisionTrace — including the honest endings REFUSED, FAILED, FATE_UNKNOWN and CONTAINED. v0.5.4 makes verification ADVERSARIAL: RFC 9381 ECVRF (vector-pinned) drives transcript-bound panel sortition nobody can grind; verdicts are sealed before any are shown; silence and fraud become witnessed, offline-verifiable evidence; and the daemon holds its first operational boundary — per-identity rate limiting whose systematic abuse is itself witnessed.

Still true, and said plainly: production forbids self-verification and never relaxes a short panel; a contained Being keeps its mind and loses only its hand; recovery restores meaning but closes unknown fates as FATE_UNKNOWN rather than inventing endings; Karma remains a reference sandbox; retrieval is a baseline, not a knowledge graph; GPU engine acceptance is an operator-run gate; the challenge round is an in-process protocol — its network transport is not built yet, and authorization policy beyond rate limiting is still absent. A reference organism is not yet a production Being.
